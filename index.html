What we do

Storage and fulfilment, handled for you

Storage

Your stock is kept safe, organised and ready to go, with space that can grow as you do.

Receiving & inventory

We check in your deliveries and keep track of your stock, so you know what you have on hand.

Pick & pack

Every order is packed by hand with care. Fragile items get extra protection, and we can use your own branded packaging.

Shipping

We send your orders out with tracked delivery, so you and your customers can follow them to the door.

Returns

Returned items are checked over and either put back into stock or set aside, depending on what you prefer.

Works with your shop

Selling on Shopify or another platform? We'll work out the simplest way to get your orders to us.
